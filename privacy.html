Who we share it with

  • Stripe, our payment provider, which processes payments and helps prevent fraud. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Our website host and email provider, which store and deliver our website and emails.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on our shop page and receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Our accountants and HMRC, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep order and payment records for 6 years after the end of the financial year in which you bought, because UK tax law requires it. We keep messages for up to 2 years, unless they relate to an order.

Cookies

Our shop page does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe's checkout uses its own essential cookies to process your payment securely and prevent fraud.

Young people

Our revision products are designed for GCSE students, but purchases should be made by an adult or with a parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, and to ask us to correct it, delete it or restrict how we use it, subject to our legal obligations (for example, keeping tax records). You can also object to our use of your data. To make a request, email AbayomiShomoye@2techconsultancy.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
