Statistics: Cumulative frequency: how do you find the median and IQR? [Higher]	Median: read across at n/2. LQ at n/4, UQ at 3n/4. | IQR = UQ − LQ
Statistics: What 5 values does a box plot show? [Higher]	Minimum, lower quartile, median, upper quartile and maximum.
Statistics: Histograms: frequency density formula. [Higher]	Frequency density = frequency ÷ class width | 30 people in 10 < x ≤ 25: 30 ÷ 15 = 2
Statistics: 40 fish are marked and released. Later 50 are caught; 8 are marked. Estimate the population. [Higher]	Capture–recapture: 40 × 50 ÷ 8 = 250 fish
Exam Skills: Which formulas do you need to memorise?	Almost all of them: areas, circles, Pythagoras, SOHCAHTOA, sine and cosine rules, quadratic formula, compound interest, prism volumes. Cone, sphere and pyramid formulas are given in the question.
Exam Skills: What does 'give your answer in terms of π' mean?	Leave π in your answer, e.g. 36π. Don't multiply it out.
Exam Skills: What does 'show that' mean?	Write every step clearly. The answer is given, so the marks are for your working.
Exam Skills: What does 'estimate' mean in a calculation question?	Round every number to 1 significant figure first, then calculate.
Exam Skills: How accurate should your final answer be?	Follow the question. If it doesn't say, 3 significant figures is usually safe. Never round in the middle of working.
Exam Skills: How long should you spend per mark?	Roughly 1 minute per mark. A 4-mark question deserves about 4 minutes.
Exam Skills: 3 ways to check your answer.	Does it make sense (a person isn't 20 m tall)? Do the inverse operation. Substitute your answer back into the equation.
Exam Skills: Calculator tips that save marks.	Put brackets around negatives: (−3)². Use the fraction button. Use ANS instead of retyping rounded numbers. Check it's in degrees (D).
